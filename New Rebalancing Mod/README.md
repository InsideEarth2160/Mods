I really love this game, but there always have been some big issues when it comes to balancing. The two of them that bothered me the most were the super-useless infantry (it often feels like they instantly die even before the enemy can even pull the trigger and they do literally no damage in their short life), as well as the ultra-you'll-never-ever-defeat-them-strong aliens! Seriously, while an AI-player as UCS/ ED/ LC on medium difficulty is no match for a decent RTS-player, it will burn your base and everything it can find to the ground, no matter what, as soon as it plays as alien. Even on easy difficulty. Even when there are one or two other human players to stand at your side. The alien-AI could be the favourite enemy for some nice coop-sessions, but in that state it was no fun at all.

The "classic" rebalancing-gamemode that was added in patch 1.38 made the aliens even tougher by giving them shields (wth?), plus it only works for skirmish games when that certain mode is activated, not for the campaign or superstorm.

So I decided to create my own mod for 2160, and while originally I only planned on changing the mentioned aspects, I discovered more and more things that I felt could use some revision in the process, so at the end it turned out to be much more complex than I had in mind in the first place.
